Once a year, not once a lesson

Timetable → Calendar

Enter your timetable once, download an .ics file, and import your whole year into Google Calendar in one go. Built for a one-week timetable where different days can run to different bells.

How to get this into Google Calendar

Import is a desktop job — the mobile app can't import files. On a computer:

  1. In Google Calendar, create a new calendar first (e.g. "Timetable 2026–27"). A separate calendar means you can hide it, or delete the whole thing next year, without touching anything else.
  2. Fill in the sections below, then press Download .ics.
  3. Go to Settings → Import & export, choose the file, and pick your new calendar as the destination.
  4. Use Save setup to keep a copy of your entries — next year you load it back and only change the dates.
