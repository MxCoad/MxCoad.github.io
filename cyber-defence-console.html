Three things to remember

Hacking — getting into a system without permission. Legal when you're hired to test it (ethical), illegal when you're not (unethical).
DDoS — many computers flood one service with traffic at once, so real users can't get in. Harder to stop than a single-attacker DoS.
Brute force — trying every possible password until one works. Beaten by long, mixed passwords and locking accounts after failed tries.
